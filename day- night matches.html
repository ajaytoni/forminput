CRICKET GAME PLAY


Explaination


Gameplay Basics
Objective:
1.Score more runs than the opposing team.

Teams: Two teams of 11 players.
Equipment: Bat, ball, wickets (stumps and bails).
Roles: Bowler (fields) bowls to a Batsman (hits), while fielders try to get the batsman out.
Scoring: Runs are scored by hitting the ball and running between the wickets, or hitting boundaries.
