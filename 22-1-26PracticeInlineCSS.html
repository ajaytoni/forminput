Welcome Back to Cricket Studio

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of 11 players, originating in England and now globally popular, especially in Commonwealth nations, focusing on scoring runs through batting, bowling, and fielding across various formats like Test, ODI, and T20, with complex rules and deep strategy. Key aspects include the 22-yard pitch, wickets (stumps with bails), overs, dismissals (bowled, caught, LBW, etc.), and the objective of outscoring the opponent to win.
